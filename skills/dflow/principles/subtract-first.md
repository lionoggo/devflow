# 先减后加 (subtract before you add)

- Remove dead weight first, then build on the simpler base.
- Reuse an existing mechanism before adding one. A new file, state, script,
  gate, or concept must say why the existing ones cannot do it.
- One decision lives in one place; pass the result, do not repeat the choice.
- Smallest change that solves the problem; fewer lines beat elegant
  boilerplate.
- In a review, ask what can be deleted before what can be added.
